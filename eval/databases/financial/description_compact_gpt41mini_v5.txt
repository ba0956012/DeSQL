-- Table purposes:
-- order: records outgoing payment orders from accounts
-- account: bank accounts with branch location and fee frequency
-- card: credit cards linked to dispositions
-- client: bank clients with demographic info
-- disp: links clients to accounts with roles (OWNER or DISPONENT)
-- district: geographic and socio-economic data by district
-- loan: loans linked to accounts with status and payment info
-- trans: account transactions with types and operations

-- Column notes:
-- order.bank_to: recipient bank code (two-letter)
-- order.k_symbol: payment purpose (e.g. SIPO = household payment)
-- account.frequency: fee frequency in Czech (e.g. POPLATEK MESICNE = monthly fee)
-- disp.type: disposition role (OWNER = account owner, DISPONENT = authorized user)
-- district.a2: district name (Czech)
-- district.a3: region name (English)
-- district.a10: ratio of urban inhabitants (percentage)
-- district.a11: average salary
-- district.a12, a13: unemployment rates for 1995 and 1996
-- district.a14: entrepreneurs per 1000 inhabitants
-- district.a15, a16: crimes committed in 1995 and 1996
-- loan.status: loan repayment status (A=finished OK, B=finished unpaid, C=running OK, D=in debt)
-- trans.type: transaction direction (PRIJEM = credit, VYDAJ = withdrawal)
-- trans.operation: transaction mode (e.g. VKLAD = cash deposit, VYBER = cash withdrawal)
